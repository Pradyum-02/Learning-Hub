01 — THE ACADEMY

ABOUT GM CRICKET
ACADEMY

GM Cricket Academy is dedicated to nurturing young talent. Our constant effort is to create and develop new players for the future.

Training runs across three branches in Pune with well-maintained grounds and high-quality wickets. More than 300 players currently receive professional training under certified coaches, with equal opportunities for boys and girls.

HEAD COACH

A WORD FROM OUR HEAD COACH

Cricket at GM is taught with discipline, patience and a clear plan for every player. We build technique first, then temperament — so young cricketers are ready when selection day arrives.

Coaching at the academy is led by certified coaches with 5+ years of experience, including former Ranji Trophy, state-level and MPL players.

Ganesh Mahapure
Head Coach, GM Cricket Academy
